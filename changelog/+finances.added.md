New Finances pages. Net worth gets Months and Movers tabs: what moved net worth each month (saving, assets, the rest), with a panel for any month or the whole range showing where money came in from and went, and how each account moved with its return. Cash flow shows income against spending, the savings rate and cash runway, with months labelled by their YNAB note or what stood out. Spending shows spending by group and category and how steady or swingy each category is. Closed accounts count for the time they held money, in the net worth history too. Settings gets a Categories tab that explains how income and spending are read and marks categories as not spending, and Net worth settings pick the accounts whose return is shown and the category of your savings goal. The budget copy now keeps split lines, categories and month notes, so the first sync after the update fetches everything again.
